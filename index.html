FAQ

Good questions

What happens if I slip?

A slip restarts your day count and nothing else. Your money saved, hours reclaimed, best streak and every badge you've earned all stay. Progress isn't all-or-nothing here.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account, no social feed and no public profile, and everything you write stays on your phone. The app sends only anonymous usage counts and purchase status to keep itself running. Your journey is yours alone.

How much does it cost?

The basics are free: the day counter, the craving button, the ride-it-out timer and the daily check-in. Pro has a 3-day free trial, then a yearly or weekly plan. Your store shows the price in your currency.

Does it work for any habit?

One habit at a time: alcohol, smoking, vaping, cannabis, gambling or your own. If it can be counted in days, Sobermark can hold it for you.

Will it nag me?

No. You choose what you get: a daily check-in reminder, a nudge the evening before a milestone, and an optional weekly nudge at your own hardest hour.

Is Sobermark medical advice?

No. It's a self-help tool, not medical care. If stopping could be risky for you, talk to a doctor first. If you're struggling, Samaritans (UK) are free on 116 123, any time.
